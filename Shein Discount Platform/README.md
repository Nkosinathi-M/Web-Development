# SHEIN Discount Platform

A real-world web development project focused on promoting SHEIN discount codes, shopping deals, and special offers while exploring digital marketing and online entrepreneurship.

## About the Project

The SHEIN Discount Platform is a personal web development project created to provide shoppers with a convenient place to discover discount codes, promotional offers, and useful links related to shopping on SHEIN.

Unlike a project built purely for practice, this website has a practical business objective: to establish an online platform that can attract visitors, provide value to shoppers, and potentially generate income through online promotional activities.

This project is also part of my Information Technology journey, allowing me to apply my web development knowledge to a real-world idea while developing the technical, entrepreneurial, and problem-solving skills needed to build and maintain useful digital products.

## How It Works

The platform presents discount codes and promotional links to visitors through a web interface.

1. Visitors open the website.
2. They browse the available discount codes and offers.
3. They copy a code where applicable.
4. They follow the associated shopping link, if provided.
5. They apply the relevant code or offer on SHEIN, subject to its terms and eligibility requirements.

Discount availability and validity may change over time. Visitors should verify the applicable conditions before completing a purchase.
